Privacy & Retention

Capital Readiness Explorer · Effective October 21, 2026

Private by default

The Capital Readiness Explorer lets you explore possible startup funding needs without creating an account or sharing your detailed financial inputs. Your entries are calculated in your own browser. Iceworks does not receive your entries just because you visit the page, change figures, or print a summary.

What you can choose to submit

If you receive an authorized personal invitation, you may choose to share a limited Capital Readiness Summary by explicitly checking the consent box and submitting. That limited summary contains only: total deployable funds, selected startup cost range, low-to-high capital gap, general financing status, and general readiness flags. We also record the invitation identifier, your consent version, and the submission date/time.

We do not collect the details you enter in the optional Financial Snapshot through this submission, including salary, account balances, property values, retirement assets, net worth, mortgage information, or debt payments. We do not request birth dates, Social Security numbers, personal addresses, account identifiers, or full credit scores through this tool.

Purpose and access

Iceworks uses submitted summaries to discuss potential franchise ownership and follow-up questions. Access is limited to authorized Iceworks employees or owners with verified sign-in and role-based permissions. We do not use these entries to promise financing or qualify you automatically for franchise ownership. We do not sell the submitted information.

Market research (PUDI)

Iceworks may preview that it has performed market research in a city or territory. Voluntarily sharing a readiness summary does not unlock proprietary PUDI scores, underlying formulas, or market reports. Iceworks decides whether to share selected market information later during an individual conversation. Market research is not a guarantee of customers, unit installations, sales, earnings, or business success.

Retention and deletion

Submitted Capital Readiness Summaries and related consent records are scheduled for automatic deletion after 90 days. Expired invitation records are eligible for deletion 30 days after expiration once any associated submission has been removed. Invitation links may be revoked sooner. Internal staff administrative action logs are kept up to 365 days. Database backups may persist for a limited additional period according to Supabase's backup settings and applicable obligations.

You can ask Iceworks to review, correct, or delete information you voluntarily submitted, subject to legal retention requirements. Please email michael@iceworksus.com with your request. Since invitation links do not necessarily verify the recipient's identity, we may ask you to verify that the record belongs to you before taking action.

Security and important limits

Invitations use randomly generated, expiring links and encrypted HTTPS connections. Submissions are stored in a private Supabase database with restricted access. An invitation link works like a bearer credential: anyone you forward it to may be able to act using it, so please keep it private. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

The Capital Readiness Explorer is an educational tool, not a loan application, legal advice, franchise offer, financing approval, financial suitability determination, or financial performance projection. Consult current franchise disclosure documents and qualified advisers before making investment decisions.
