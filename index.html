1. Build the borrower story. Capture the borrower, market, management experience, target customers, sales strategy, and revenue model.

3. Stress the projections. Compare downside, base, and upside cases, then test cash, equipment debt, break-even, and DSCR.
4. Finish the SBA package. Track lender documents, identify missing items, and print a clean projection and readiness package.

Assumption Documentation

Package limitations: Results depend entirely on assumptions entered. Actual machine placements, pricing, service costs, collections, financing terms, taxes, owner compensation, and overhead may differ materially. DSCR is calculated as EBITDA before debt service divided by modeled debt service. Lenders may use different addbacks, exclusions, global cash-flow analysis, collateral standards, and minimum thresholds.
