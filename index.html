Iceworks Franchisee Financial Model v4.2

SBA and lender-facing planning model for startup uses, machine growth, operating costs, equipment financing, cash runway, and debt-service coverage. Assumptions are editable and outcomes are estimates—not promises or guarantees.
Lender calibration · three scenarios

Underwriting Review Flow

All calculations remain in this browser
1. Document the assumptions. Choose base, downside, or upside and identify the basis for growth, overhead, service costs, and financing.
2. Model a realistic startup ramp. Machine placements and overhead build in stages rather than assuming mature operations from Month 1.
3. Stress the debt structure. Compare 60-, 84-, 96-, and 120-month equipment terms without hiding an unfavorable result.
4. Print a lender-ready summary. Use of funds, annual results, DSCR, minimum cash, and scenario comparisons are organized for review.

Projection Assumptions

Base scenario
1. Customer Leases & Machine Growth three-stage ramp
New placements may be fractional because this is an averaging model. For example, 1.5 machines per month represents 18 placements over a year.
2. Startup Uses & Sources SBA request
The working-capital reserve and extra cash cushion remain available after launch. Other startup uses are assumed paid at closing.
3. Machine & Equipment Financing cohort loans
Financing terms are planning assumptions only. Confirm available advance rates, APR, amortization, collateral requirements, and payment timing with the actual lender.
4. Cleaning, Repairs & Parts service reserve
Service reserves remain provisional until Iceworks completes the current cost-of-sales account mapping.
5. Startup Overhead Ramp scale with business
The mature-overhead target may reflect an established operation, but the startup amount should reflect the actual early operating structure—not a mature fleet from Day 1.
6. Assumption Documentation lender support
This lender-facing build intentionally does not embed an established-operator or Colorado performance preset. Any actual outlet-performance information should be handled consistently with the current FDD and reviewed by franchise counsel.
Important: This model is a planning and discussion tool. It does not constitute a financial performance representation, earnings claim, loan approval, accounting opinion, tax advice, or guarantee by Iceworks Franchise Group, LLC. The borrower and lender should validate every assumption and consult appropriate professional advisors.

Assumption Documentation

Model limitations: Results depend entirely on assumptions entered. Actual machine placements, pricing, service costs, collections, financing terms, taxes, owner compensation, and overhead may differ materially. DSCR is calculated as EBITDA before debt service divided by modeled debt service. Lenders may use different addbacks, exclusions, global cash-flow analysis, collateral standards, and minimum thresholds.
