What this means—and what it doesn't

Capital affects what you may be able to fund, not how many customers you will obtain. This tool does not forecast sales, leasing income, profits, cash flow, return on investment, or equipment payback. Actual requirements vary by territory, financing terms, timing, service costs, taxes, and the governing Franchise Disclosure Document. A larger net worth does not necessarily mean more spendable capital.
