    What this tool can—and cannot—tell you

    It compares stated funding sources with published startup costs and user-selected extra expenses. It does not predict customers, machine placements, leasing revenue, profits, payback, cash flow, investment returns or franchise success. Net worth isn't necessarily spendable cash. Actual expenses, financing conditions and franchise terms may differ. Consult the governing Franchise Disclosure Document, your lender and your professional advisers.
